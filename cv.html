Projects

Machine-learning prediction of a proton-irradiated lead-free solar cellCourse project

With two teammates, supervised by Dr. S. M. Sadakatul Bari. Ran the radiation chain for an ITO/C60/Rb₂AgInBr₆/Cu₂NiSnS₄/Pt cell in the ISS orbit: SPENVIS (AP9 protons), MULASSIS (5 mm glass) and SRIM/TRIM gave a radiation defect density of 1.59 × 10⁷ cm⁻³, added in SCAPS-1D. Produced 4,212 simulated devices (efficiency 1.5–37%) and trained 5 regression models: CatBoost reached a test R² of 0.9999 and a 0.48% efficiency error on 48 unseen devices; SHAP showed defect and acceptor density dominate. The model fails outside its range, and the data are simulated only.

AeroSoar: vertical air velocity estimation for soaring glidersSimulation study

First author, with two co-authors, of an open-source simulator (Python and JavaScript, MIT licence) to estimate vertical air velocity and detect thermals for autonomous soaring gliders. Compared causal filters and detectors (Kalman, extended Kalman, particle filters, CUSUM) over 30 simulated flights per condition, across 3 mission types and with sensor faults. In simulation, substituting the true speed rate for the Kalman filter’s estimate cut its error in speed changes from 0.70 to 0.05 m/s. Replayed 53 minutes of recorded flight data (public ALFA dataset) to check the sensor assumptions. Reproducible from fixed seeds; simulation only, with no claim of flight readiness.
