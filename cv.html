Projects

AeroSoar: vertical air velocity estimation for soaring glidersSimulation study

Open-source simulation environment (Python and JavaScript, MIT licence) for causal signal processing to estimate vertical air velocity and detect thermals in autonomous soaring gliders. Compares causal filters and detectors, including Kalman, extended Kalman and particle filters and CUSUM, under sensor faults and across missions. In simulation, substituting the true speed rate for the Kalman filter’s estimate cut its error during speed changes from 0.70 to 0.05 m/s. Results are reproducible from fixed seeds; simulation only, with no claim of flight readiness. Code · Live simulator

Solar panel selection for the PicoSat, PicoSat4BangladeshTrade study

Compared three commercial 1U CubeSat solar panels (Spectrolab XTE-SF, Aphelion 1U, EnduroSat 1U Solar-Z) on efficiency, power, temperature range, sensors and price, and recommended the Aphelion panel for the satellite’s power system.
